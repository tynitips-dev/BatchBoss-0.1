Read the first-recipe checklist +
  1. Open the web app. Sign in or choose Create an account.
  2. Gather pack details. Keep each ingredient’s name, price, quantity and unit handy. For example: flour, R50, 1 kg.
  3. Save your ingredients. Check the units and pack quantities carefully before adding them to a recipe.
  4. Add a recipe. Choose each ingredient and enter the quantity used. Set the batch yield so per-item costing is meaningful.
  5. Review before quoting. Include packaging, labour and overheads when deciding your selling price. Use your own figures rather than the demo values.

A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before your
first bake.

Still have a question?
We’re here to help ↘

Can I use BatchBoss on my computer?+

Yes. Open app.batchboss.co.za in your browser to sign in or create a bakery account. You can explore the web portal without waiting for the wider Android release.

Is the Android app available?+

Android early-access testing is available by invitation. Email us with your Google Play email address to ask about joining the tester list. Wider release details will be announced when ready.

Can I import an Excel ingredient list?+

The web app has an ingredient-list upload workflow. Use the BatchBoss template in Documents & Templates, check your pack prices, quantities and units, and review the import preview before confirming. Contact support if your columns do not match.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?+

Free is the starting point for recipe and ingredient costing. Pro adds document tools such as invoices, quotes and receipts. Check your account’s Subscription section or contact us for current pricing, limits and access.

Can I use my app account on the website?+

Use the same email address when signing in to the web portal. If a record looks different between devices, contact support before re-importing it so we can help check the account and sync status.

Does the calculator show my final selling price?+

No. The homepage calculator shows the cost of a single ingredient quantity. To choose a selling price, also account for the rest of your ingredients, packaging, labour, overheads and profit.

How do I get help?+

Email info@batchboss.co.za or use WhatsApp below. For technical issues, include your device, the relevant screen and what happened. Please do not send passwords or private customer information.
