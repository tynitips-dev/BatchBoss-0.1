Privacy policy

Effective 17 September 2026 · Website and early-access enquiries

Scope

This policy covers the BatchBoss website, support enquiries and early-access tester list. BatchBoss is currently coming soon. Information about processing within the app will be published before app testing or launch; this website policy does not describe or verify the app’s data handling.

Information you provide

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, including your name if you provide it. If you join the tester list, we receive your name and Google Play email address. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary customer or sensitive information.

How we use information

We use support messages to respond to enquiries and troubleshoot reported issues. We use tester-list details to manage early-access enquiries, invitations and testing-related communications. Joining the tester list is voluntary and does not subscribe you to unrelated marketing.

Providers and website requests

Your email is handled by the email services used to deliver and receive it. The website host processes technical requests needed to display the website and may maintain server logs. This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the technical request needed to supply those fonts. The website does not include an analytics script, advertising tracker, account-registration form or payment form.

Testing invitations

Your Google Play email address may be used to invite you through Google Play’s testing programme when testing becomes available. We will explain any additional app data processing before testing begins. Joining the list does not guarantee access, and a public review is optional.

Retention and your requests

We keep support correspondence while needed to handle the enquiry and any related follow-up, and tester-list details while needed for early-access invitations and testing follow-up, subject to legal retention requirements. You may leave the tester list or request access, correction or deletion by emailing info@batchboss.co.za. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Removal from the tester list does not itself delete any future app account.

Security and complaints

Avoid sending confidential information in an ordinary support email. Report concerns to info@batchboss.co.za. You may also contact South Africa’s Information Regulator about personal-information complaints. Applicable statutory rights remain available.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when website information-handling practices change. For support and privacy enquiries, email info@batchboss.co.za.
