How Data Deletion Works

Under Google Play Developer Policy and our privacy commitments, BatchBoss provides users with full autonomy over their personal and bakery data. You can delete your account and all associated data through two methods:

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

Open the BatchBoss app > navigate to More > tap Account & Data Deletion > tap Delete My Account & Data Immediately. Your data is instantly and permanently wiped from your device and local storage.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

Submit the form below or email us directly at info@batchboss.co.za. Our administrative team will process the deletion within 48 hours.

Data Types Subject to Deletion

When you request deletion of your BatchBoss account, the following data categories will be permanently removed:

  • User Profile: Name, email address, bakery name, phone number, operating model, location, and authentication credentials.
  • Bakery Recipes & Formulas: All created recipes, ingredient ratios, batch sizes, cost calculations, and pricing margins.
  • Inventory & Stock: Ingredient stock levels, unit purchase prices, low-stock thresholds, and custom suppliers.
  • Financial Records: Quotes, invoices, custom line items, tax settings, and profit calculations.
  • Customer Directory: Client names, phone numbers, delivery addresses, notes, and order histories.
  • Audit & Login Logs: Device logs, session records, and task lists.

Notice on Data Retention: Once deleted, your account and recipes cannot be recovered. Data required to be retained under statutory financial or tax laws (e.g. issued fiscal invoices) will be kept only as strictly mandated by law.
