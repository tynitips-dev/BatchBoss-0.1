Data policy

Effective 17 September 2026 · Website and early-access enquiries

Scope

This policy covers information received through the BatchBoss website’s support and tester-list email links. It does not set out the upcoming app’s storage, scanning, backup or account-deletion behaviour. App-specific information will be added before testing or launch.

Website information

The website displays app information and screenshots. It does not offer an account login, collect recipes or customer records, or operate a website database of baking-business information. Email buttons open your email app; they do not submit information automatically.

Support and tester-list records

Information you send by email is held in the receiving mailbox and, for testing invitations, the tester list. We use these records for support and early-access communications. Provide only the details needed for your enquiry or invitation.

Screenshots and examples

The screenshots show the app in development. Example prices and figures are illustrative. Do not rely on screenshot figures as business advice or evidence of your own account’s records.

Leaving the list and deletion requests

Email info@batchboss.co.za to leave the tester list, correct your contact information or request deletion of information you sent to us. Identity verification and applicable legal retention requirements may affect a request. Website enquiries do not create an app account.

Future app records

Before using the app, read the app-specific privacy and data information published for that release. Keep independent copies of important business records and do not assume storage, backups or deletion behaviour from this website policy.

Contact

For data questions or suspected misuse, email info@batchboss.co.za. Do not include passwords in your report.
