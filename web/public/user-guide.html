Customer user guide · 15 October 2026

A beginner-friendly overview based on the current web-app source. Screen labels and payment offers may change. Always verify figures and customer details before sending documents.

Open BatchBoss appGet support

1. Create an account or sign in

Visit app.batchboss.co.za. Sign in with your existing account or choose Create an account. Use the same email as the Android app to avoid creating a second workspace.

2. Set up Business settings

Open Business settings, enter bakery contact and banking details, upload a logo, select the invoice/document accent color, set the labor rate and save.

3. Ingredients and stock

Add each ingredient with its pack price, pack quantity, measurement unit, current stock and stock alert level. Verify the calculated cost per unit.

4. Packaging

Record boxes, boards and other packaging using real pack costs and quantities. Keep supplier and inventory details current.

5. Recipes and costing

Create a recipe, add ingredients and quantities, then labor, overheads, utilities, packaging and target profit margin. Review batch cost and cost per unit before saving.

6. Customers

Use Customer Directory to save contact, delivery, preferences and allergy information. Confirm allergies with customers for each order.

7. Orders and calendar

Track orders, production tasks, dates and priorities. Invoices can create linked orders and preparation tasks.

8. Documents, sync and reports

Download starter spreadsheets in Documents & templates. Review imported data, use Sync now to refresh cloud data, and export available reports.

9. Pro subscription

In Subscription, read prices and renewal terms carefully before choosing a Paystack plan. Confirm that Pro is active after payment.

10. Products and services

In Pro, enter the items you sell, their cost, selling price, unit and optional SKU. Reuse these products in quotes and invoices.

11. Quotes and estimates

Choose quote or estimate, select a customer, add catalog or custom lines, discounts and applicable VAT, then verify the total before sharing.

12. Invoices

Add a client, due date, products, optional packaging and notes. Generate, review, then use View / Save PDF. Mark paid only after receiving payment.

13. Receipts and suppliers

Record receipts against payments and keep supplier details up to date. Options can differ between Android and web versions.

14. Troubleshooting

If records appear missing, confirm the bakery account and use Sync now. If costing looks incorrect, check units and pack sizes. For payment issues, contact info@batchboss.co.za with the transaction reference, never card details.
